# A journey with no ceremony in it

<!-- vocab-lint:journey -->

| Step | Who acts | What happens | What you must create |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | You | Install Mesh. | Nothing |
| 2 | You | Point it at a folder. | Nothing |
| 3 | You | Connect an agent. | Nothing |
| 4 | Mesh | Gives each actor its own private view. | Nothing |
| 5 | Agents | Read and write ordinary files. | Nothing |
| 6 | Mesh | Saves each actor's stable work privately. | Nothing |
| 7 | Mesh | Makes stable private work available to team. | Nothing |
| 8 | Mesh | Raises a review card when an actor settles. | Nothing |
| 9 | You | Open the card and read what changed. | Nothing |
| 10 | You | Approve all of it, or only part of it. | Nothing |
| 11 | Mesh | Checks the approval against the shared version. | Nothing |
| 12 | Mesh | Advances the shared version in one step. | Nothing |
